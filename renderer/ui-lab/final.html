Never (review checklist — ui-kit.md §6)

  • No pills. No 999 px radius, no tinted capsule for status, filters, counts, badges or modes. Status = lamp (+ word).
  • No card in a card. Paper never in paper; board never in paper. A board holds papers side by side, never stacked.
  • One clay per surface. Clay = selection + the one primary button. No second accent, no status-coloured buttons.
  • Status colour only on lamps, readouts, the alarm tally cell, stamps, the blocked group header and question margins.
  • No boxed rows or groups. Dividers are the groove (board) or the dashed stitch (paper).
  • No buttons in rows. Rows show a key legend on the selected row only.
  • No ALL CAPS outside plaques, group headers, stamps and the dot-matrix. Buttons, states, labels: sentence case.
  • No backdrop-filter, no neutral-black shadows, nothing drawn over terminal glyphs.
  • Blocked is said at most 3× per view: HUD alarm cell, the blocked group header, the drawer counter (+ the world).
  • Keys are always keycaps (no "[E]" text, no ⌘ on Linux); Leader shown once per legend.
