# M1 carryover (round-3 reviewer findings; round-3 fixes were applied after these but NOT re-reviewed)

## score 7.5
- [BRN] A done agent walking to the sofa uses the laptop-typing walk (brain.js done() sets intent.walkActivity='walkType'). That arm pose means 'working', so the signal is dishonest. It also ambles at 0.9 m/s: gale was still walking at 12 s after the status change (tr_done-08..15). Use a plain happy skip (or null / a done variant) for done and idle walks, keep walkType for working scurries only, and bring the done walk to about ≥1.4 m/s with a bouncier gait.
- [BRN] A walker that starts inside the avoid radius walks straight through the player. I stood the camera 1.5 m from gale's desk and forced done; gale hopped up and walked into the lens, with the hat filling the frame (tr_done-10/11, well under 0.6 m). The sidestep/routeAround logic only covers passing walkers, not a walker whose start point or first leg is within avoidR of the player. Plan the first leg away from the player, or hold the victory wave until the player clears. The soft collider (PLY) should also resolve actor-into-player, not only player-into-actor.
- [CHR] The waveBlocked 'hey!' arm at the desk is still a short nub that barely clears the shoulder (f_status_blocked-*, tr_blocked-04..15). From proto it reads much weaker than the new queueHandUp arm. Give waveBlocked the same long, over-the-head reach with overshoot on each wave peak so blocked-at-desk reads from across the room.
- [BRN] The work-call scurry (done→working) keeps the 'happy' face for about 2.4 s while running back to the desk at 2.8 m/s (anim.face=happy while status=working, walkActivity walkType). One visible change should flip it: jolt/startle, then a determined or focused face for the whole scurry, not a happy smile carried over from done.

At the focus_status_done distance they read as eyebrows on a blank face (crop_done.png), which breaks the rule that eyes are never under 1/9 of body width and always read. From the entrance pose they look stuck on top of the chair. Either lower or thin the backrest, or move the seat anchor forward about 0.08–0.1 m.
