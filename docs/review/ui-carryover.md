# Ideas
- Toast folding priority (hud): when three tickets stack, the oldest folds to its headline even if it is the warn. 'Closing panes needs allowMutations' loses its 'off in the default session' line, while 'Copied 3 lines' stays open (mixed1280-35, mixed1600-35). Rank by level (blocked > warn/error > done > info) instead of by age.
- Blocked wording differs across surfaces for the same state: roster 'Blocked ≥ 0:46' (red text + triangle), triage 'Waiting ≥ 0:52', and the inbox and status card 'WAITING' rubber stamp. Pick one word (Waiting or Blocked) for the time-since label everywhere; keep the stamp-on-paper vs red text on wood split.
- Prompt bar legend uses '[Alt] + [1]' with a literal plus, while every other chord in the kit is shown as adjacent caps ('[Ctrl `][U]', '[⇧][B]'). Drop the '+' for consistency.
- Drawer footer notice 'Cropped · pane is 100×50 · yours is 14 px' is cryptic ('yours is 14 px' = font size?). Consider 'at 14px font' or dropping it.
- Drawer header truncates the cwd to '~/src/c…' at 1280 even though the header has room (mixed1280-08). Let the path take the slack before the context meter.
- Away recap ledger lines ellipsize ('is waiting on you, and 3 more in the q…') inside a sheet with room to wrap to two lines (mixed1280/1600-26). Wrap or shorten the copy.
- Roster focused row draws both the clay left bar and a rounded outline ring (mixed1600-43 willow, mixed1280-27 claude·2). One focus mark is enough; the ring reads as a card inside the list.
- Collapsed drawer rail bottom: the 'blocked' word runs edge to edge of the 44px rail and the 'Ctrl `' cap is stacked over 'U'. Consider dropping the word (the triangle + dot-matrix count already says it) or rotating it like 'TERMINALS · 1'.
- Settings: in mixed1280-41 four ']' presses at 80ms left the sheet on 'Look' while one press worked (mixed1280-40). Worth checking that rapid bracket presses don't drop focus out of the sheet (a flint status card also appeared behind it).
- Keys overlay at 720p cuts the right column at the bottom with no fade or scroll hint, unlike Help, which has a fade. Reuse the same bottom fade.
- Hotbar name labels tint blocked names red. The cubby's lamp already carries the state, so keep the labels in neutral ink (§4.2 does not list names).
- Status card for a done agent: the clay 'Sign off' primary has no key tile, while the legend says 'G high-five'. Put [G] on the button and drop it from the legend (rule 5: a button or a legend entry, never both).
- Recap slip sentences get ellipsised at 1600 ('and 2 more in the que…'). Let them wrap to 2 lines; it is the agent-voice serif and should read in full.
- Settings 'Look' section leaves a large empty paper area under three controls. Size the sheet to its section, or keep a min-height but pull the legend up.
- Roster: the focus outline on an expanded blocked row boxes only the name+question block, while the options and the row legend hang below outside it. Either outline the whole expanded row or rely on the selection bar alone.
- Fullscreen Peek (mixed1600-29): a 100x50 pane at 11px fills only the left ~40% of a very wide glass. The drawer owner already noted it does not refit. Worth doing for the 'feels like a real CRT' read.
- Overall the kit is now coherent across surfaces: walnut boards, cream clamped paper, enamel plaques, keycaps, lamps, stamps, tickets and the printout all read as one carpenter's fittings. The remaining issues are about collisions and density at 1280, not material language.
- Verified green: npm test 779 pass / 0 fail / 1 skip; p2.mjs 50/50 on port 7828. Tab,Enter reaches a focused xterm on demo, crowd40 and hqtest. Digits 1-5 and Leader flows work, and Enter still works after closing a dialog with Esc. The drawer menus are keyboard-driven, and Esc returns focus to the xterm.
